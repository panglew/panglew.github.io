EECS 494: Project 2 - Rapid Development

Languages & Tools Used: C#, Unity, GitHub, JIRA

  • This project began as a rapid prototype developed over the course of 1 week, designed to quickly test core gameplay mechanics. Through iterative development, I incorporated player feedback from a gameplay testing session to refine the prototype into this final product. The process emphasized adaptability, continuous improvement, and user-centered design.
  • Overall timeline: 2 weeks
  • This project furthered my skills in programming and game development, as well as increased my understanding of player guidance and playtesting.
